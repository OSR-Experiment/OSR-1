Blop v30 map

Every page, every knob and every hidden shortcut on your FM-1. Each page has the colour the device shows for it.

Page with its own buttonHidden: needs a combo
HOME+GLO means hold the first, tap the second.Press a page button again to go back to MAIN.

The pages

Each page has 4 settings on knobs K1 to K4. Turn a knob and the value shows on the screen.

The 5 big knobs

SelectChord grid, 38 grids. Used when SCALE is GRID.
AlgorithmRhythm, 16. OFF: the chord sounds while held. A rhythm: the chord keeps going (latch), the same key again stops it.
PresetSound, 48 in 6 families: Pads, Keys, Plucks, Experimental, Analog, Dark wave. Same as HOME K1.
MasterVolume.

Hidden: combos with HOME

HOME+GLO
DRUMS 2: Rim, Clap, Tom, Cowbell. Again = back to DRUMS.
HOME+ENV
GROOVE 2: Strum. Again = back to GROOVE.
HOME+SCL
Scale on/off. GRID and your last scale swap.
HOME+LFO
MIDI MAP: STANDARD, then KEYSTEP 37, then OFF.
HOME+EDIT
MIDI channel: ALL, 1, 2 ... 16.
HOME+OCT+
CPU meter: load, peak, dropouts (XRUNS).
Hold HOME
Help screen.
Hold HOME+REC 3 s
Reset to my defaults. Let go early to cancel.

Songs: 12 memories

SAVE+hold a white key
Saves the whole setup and loop into that song. Keys C4 to G5 are songs 1 to 12. Hold 1.5 s. Green banner: SAVED.
SAVE+tap a white key
Loads that song. Blue banner: LOADED. Press PLAY for its loop. Grey banner: EMPTY.
SAVE alone
The creature in its bowl, the music heard from outside.

Settings also save themselves 4 s after your last change, when no chord is playing.

Looper and keys

REC
Start recording. Play chords, press REC again to finish. REC blinks while recording.
PLAY
Play or stop the loop. With no loop: stop or bring back the chord.
OCT- OCT+
Octave from -2 to +2.
27 keys
Chords. GRID: the grid's chords. A scale: the key is the root.
